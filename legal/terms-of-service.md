## 1. What Vatulo is

Vatulo helps people find house parties and choose who comes to theirs. We
provide the software. **We do not host, organise, supervise, endorse or insure
any party.** Parties are private events run by the people who create them.

## 2. You must be 18

Vatulo is for adults only. By using it you confirm you are 18 or older. We
delete accounts belonging to anyone younger.

## 3. Your account

Give accurate information and keep your password to yourself. One account per
person. You are responsible for what happens under your account. Do not
impersonate anyone, and do not use somebody else's photos.

## 4. Content you post

You keep ownership of your photos, messages and profile. You give us a licence
to store and display them inside Vatulo so the product can work — nothing wider.
That licence ends when you delete the content or your account, except for copies
we must keep for safety or legal reasons.

## 5. Things you may not do

**Zero tolerance. There is no objectionable content on Vatulo and no abusive
users.** Specifically, you may not:

- Harass, threaten, stalk, or intimidate anyone.
- Post sexual content involving minors, or any sexual content involving someone
  who has not consented — including intimate images shared without consent.
- Post hate speech, or attack people over race, ethnicity, national origin,
  religion, disability, sex, gender, sexual orientation, or age.
- Post violence, gore, or content promoting self-harm.
- Advertise, sell, or solicit — including drugs, weapons, or sex work.
- Impersonate anyone, or post someone else's photos as your own.
- Share anyone's address, phone number, or other private details without their
  permission. **This includes a party's address.**
- Scrape, automate, or reverse-engineer the service.
- Serve alcohol to anyone under the legal drinking age at a party you host.

## 6. Reporting, and what we do about it

Every profile and every message can be reported from inside the app. Every
report is reviewed. **We aim to act on reports of objectionable content within
24 hours**, and we may remove content, suspend an account, or ban it
permanently, with or without notice.

You can block any user at any time. Blocking is immediate and mutual: neither of
you will see the other.

Hosts approve every photo before it appears in a party's recap.

## 7. Safety, and the limits of what we can promise

**This is the part that matters most, so it is written plainly.**

Vatulo connects people who do not already know each other, and parties happen in
private homes. We reduce risk where we can: addresses are hidden until a host
accepts you and you commit — and hosts may hold them back further still, until a
day, three hours, or an hour before the party — hosts choose their guests one by
one, everyone is 18+, profiles carry reputation from past parties, and anyone can
be reported or blocked.

We cannot make it safe. **We do not run background checks. We do not verify
identity. We do not vet homes.** Verification on Vatulo currently means a
confirmed email address, and nothing more — the badge on a profile says that an
email was confirmed, not that a person is who they claim to be. We may begin
requiring a confirmed phone number as well; if we do, it raises the cost of
holding several accounts and does nothing to establish who anybody is.

You are responsible for your own judgement about who to meet and whose home to
enter. Tell someone where you are going. Leave if you are uncomfortable.

Hosts are responsible for their guests, their property, and for obeying the law
where they live — including the law on serving alcohol.

## 8. Suspension

We may suspend or terminate an account that breaks these terms, or where we
reasonably believe someone is at risk. Where we can, we will say why.

error-free, or that anyone you meet through it is who they say they are.
