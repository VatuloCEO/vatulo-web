How it works

Your next night starts with a swipe.

STEP 01

Discover your night

Swipe through house parties happening around you. See the vibe, who's going, how far it is, and whether people from your Crowd are already connected.

STEP 02

Make your move

Like a party and your application goes to the host. If the host already picked you too, you're instantly in. If someone from your Crowd invites you directly, you'll see it right away.

STEP 03

Show up

Once you're accepted and commit, Vatulo gives you the party details when the host chooses to release them. Then all that's left is to show up, meet people, and make the night worth remembering.

Why Vatulo

More than finding a party.

Parties picked for you

Your Discover deck learns what you actually like — your distance, interests, party vibe, social overlap and the kinds of nights you keep choosing.

Build your Crowd

Meet someone you want to see again? Add them to your Crowd. Message each other, find people in common, invite them out and watch your real-world social circle grow.

A reputation that means something

Show up when you say you will. Be someone hosts want back. Throw nights guests would attend again. Your reputation comes from what actually happens offline.

Sometimes the party picks you

Hosts discover guests too. If a host invites you through Discover, their party can be waiting somewhere in your deck. Pick each other and it's an instant match.

The night doesn't disappear

After the party, checked-in guests can contribute photos to the recap. Approved moments stay with the people who were actually there.

Bring your people

Some nights are better together. See who from your Crowd is already going, invite them to the nights you host, and keep a list of the people you always want there.

Trust & safety

Strangers, in somebody's home. We take that seriously.

We cannot make meeting new people risk-free, and we won't pretend to. What we can do is make sure nobody gets in without a host's decision, and that the app itself never becomes the thing that exposes you.

  • Everyone is 18 or over. A date of birth is required at sign-up, and we delete accounts belonging to anyone younger.
  • Hosts approve every guest, individually. There is no open door and no way to buy your way onto a list.
  • Your location never leaves your phone. Distances are worked out on the device. We do not transmit, store or log your coordinates — there is no column in our database that holds them.
  • Addresses are released, not published. Only to guests who committed, and only when the host says so.
  • Block and report anyone, from anywhere. Blocking is immediate and mutual. We aim to act on reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.
  • Your gender is never shown on its own. It appears only inside a party's aggregate ratio, and only once at least five guests are on the list.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
