Not a feed of events. A door that opens.

Ticketed events are for strangers in a room. House parties are for people someone vouched for — which is why they are hard to find if you don't already know the right person. Vatulo is the way in: real parties near you, hosted by people who choose their guests one at a time.

How it works

Three steps, and none of them is buying a ticket.

STEP 01

See what's on

A deck of parties near you — tonight, this weekend, the kind of night you actually like. Swipe through it, save what looks good, pass on what doesn't. Hosts never see that you passed.

STEP 02

Ask to come

Applying takes one tap. The host sees your profile, your reputation from parties you've already been to, and how many friends you have in common — then decides. It is their home.

STEP 03

Get the address

Accepted, then you commit. The exact address arrives on the host's schedule — the moment you commit, or a day, three hours, or one hour before the door opens. Until then, nobody outside the list has it.

Inside the app

Built around the things that decide whether a night is good.

A deck with an opinion

The order is not chronological and it is not random. What you've said you like, how far it is, who's going, how fresh it is — the parties most likely to be your night come first.

Your Crowd

The people you actually meet. Add them after a party, message them directly, and see how many of them a host already knows before you apply. Leaving a Crowd is one tap, and it is mutual.

Reputation you earned

Said you'd come and came. Hosts who'd have you back. Guests who'd return to your place. It builds from real nights, and it is what gets you into the next one.

Invitations that find you

A host can invite you straight to a party, even one you'd never have seen. It arrives quietly in your deck rather than as a name-dropping notification — you decide on the party, not on who asked.

The morning after

Photos from the night land in a recap only the people who were there can see — and the host approves every one before it appears. Nothing goes up because somebody else pressed a button.

Who's actually coming

How full a party is, and the balance of the room, before you commit to it. Shown as a ratio and only once enough people are on the list — never attached to anybody by name.

Trust & safety

Strangers, in somebody's home. We take that seriously.

We cannot make meeting new people risk-free, and we won't pretend to. What we can do is make sure nobody gets in without a host's decision, and that the app itself never becomes the thing that exposes you.

  • Everyone is 18 or over. A date of birth is required at sign-up, and we delete accounts belonging to anyone younger.
  • Hosts approve every guest, individually. There is no open door and no way to buy your way onto a list.
  • Your location never leaves your phone. Distances are worked out on the device. We do not transmit, store or log your coordinates — there is no column in our database that holds them.
  • Addresses are released, not published. Only to guests who committed, and only when the host says so.
  • Block and report anyone, from anywhere. Blocking is immediate and mutual. We aim to act on reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.
  • Your gender is never shown on its own. It appears only inside a party's aggregate ratio, and only once at least five guests are on the list.

The full detail is in the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
